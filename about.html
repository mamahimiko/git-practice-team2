Get to know us

We are a group of students passionate about making GitHub easier to understand for beginners. When we first started learning version control, we found it confusing and sometimes overwhelming, so we decided to create something that would help others avoid the same struggles.

Our goal is to build clear, simple, and beginner-friendly manual that guides new users through GitHub step by step. We focus on explaining the essentials in plain languge, with examples that are easy to follow and relate to real student projects. Whether you're just creating your first repository or trying to understand branches and merges, we're here to help you gain confidence and enjoy the learning process.
